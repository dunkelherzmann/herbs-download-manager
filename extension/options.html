Naming Rules

How the videos and sounds a site plays are named and saved, and what a click in the list does there. Without a rule, a video is named after its page's title.

No rules yet.

In the name: %title the page's title, %site the site's name (youtube), %selector the text of the page element the selector finds (such as h1 or .channel-name). The extension (.mp4, .mp3…) is added.
